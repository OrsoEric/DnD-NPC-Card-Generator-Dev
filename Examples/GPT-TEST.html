Description and History

The Flail of Submission and Shame is a nine-cat tail made from entwined thick rope. At each end of this macabre appendage, wicked butcher hooks lie in wait, poised to tear into flesh upon impact.

The rope’s length allows the wielder to reach their own back, inflicting both physical pain and emotional humiliation.

The flail was given to the Slagborn, a group of laborers working in the underground tunnels beneath Waterdeep, by a cruel master who found amusement in their suffering.

Over time, the flail absorbed the resilience and determination of the Slagborn, becoming a symbol of their enduring spirit and will to survive.
